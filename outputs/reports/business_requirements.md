## 1. BUSINESS PROBLEM & ORGANIZATIONAL CONTEXT

Vireo Audio is experiencing rapid direct-to-consumer and omnichannel growth. However, customer experience leadership and financial controllership are deadlocked over support staffing, capacity planning, and queue allocation:

1. **The Hiring Debate (The ₹9 Lakh Question):**
   - Headcount budget is available for **two additional hires** (~₹9,00,000 annual fully loaded cost, or ₹4.5L per agent).
   - **Priya Raman's Position:** Intended to award both hires to **Billing**, citing the helpdesk intake chart showing Billing as the single largest queue by volume (2,564 tickets, 21.8% of all tickets).
   - **Neha Kulkarni's Position:** Argues that Billing is over-reported because of misrouting, while **Logistics is actually drowning** with resolution times exceeding 24 hours, heavy hand-offs, and tanking satisfaction.
   - **Arjun Mehta's Position:** Demands an ironclad financial and data-driven business case before authorizing ₹9L in recurring payroll, specifically asking whether process re-engineering and bot routing fixes can eliminate the need for headcount expansion.

2. **The Root Operational Dilemma:**
   - Intake tags are generated by a brittle chatbot flow and rarely corrected by agents.
   - Inbound volume by department reflects **bot routing rules**, NOT true customer intent.
   - 795 tickets (31%) routed to Billing were quietly transferred to and resolved by Logistics.
   - Without an accurate re-classification of customer intent, Vireo risks allocating capital to the wrong department while leaving customer churn drivers unaddressed.

## 2. PROPOSED CATEGORY TAXONOMY APPROACH

### 2.1. Deficiencies in the Current Intake Taxonomy
The existing 11 bot categories exhibit severe semantic overlap and operational ambiguity:
- **"Billing & Payments" vs. "Delivery & Shipping":** Customers complaining that "I paid for my order but it hasn't arrived" are systematically dumped into Billing.
- **"Other" Dump Category:** 1,622 tickets (13.8%) are tagged "Other", hiding thousands of fulfillment delays, address corrections, and cancellation requests.
- **Fragmented Technical Issues:** Hardware/software issues are fragmented into 5 narrow buckets (`Connectivity`, `Charging & Battery`, `App & Firmware`, `Audio Quality`, `Account & Login`) without distinguishing Tier 1 resets from Tier 2 hardware defects.

### 2.2. Proposed 7-Pillar Intent-Based Taxonomy
To drive accurate routing and root-cause analysis, we propose reclassifying all tickets into a clean, MECE (Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive) taxonomy:

```
                            VIREO SUPPORT INTENT TAXONOMY
                                          │
       ┌──────────────────┬───────────────┴───────────────┬──────────────────┐
       │                  │                               │                  │
Order & Fulfillment  Returns & Refunds              Product & Tech     Billing & Invoicing
  - Order Tracking     - Return Pickup                - Connectivity     - Payment Gateways
  - Shipping Delay     - QC Status Inspection         - Battery/Charging - Double Debits
  - Courier / Address  - Refund Processing            - App / Firmware   - Invoice / Tax
  - Damaged in Transit                                - Audio Quality
                                                                             │
                          ┌───────────────────────────────┴──────────────────┘
                          │                               │
                Warranty & Hardware Claims      Account & Pre-Sales
                  - Multi-touch RMA Diagnostics   - Login / OTP
                  - Out-of-Box DOA Replacement    - Specifications
                  - Service Center Escalations    - Compatibility
```

1. **Order Logistics & Shipping:** Delivery status, tracking delays, courier handover, address modifications, lost in transit.
2. **Returns & Reverse Logistics:** Return requests, pickup scheduling, transit QC, return policy inquiries.
3. **Billing, Payments & Invoicing:** Failed/duplicate transactions, invoice downloads, GST credit, coupon/price discrepancies.
4. **Technical Support & Firmware (Tier 1 Frontline):** Bluetooth pairing, reset procedures, firmware updates, app crashes, audio troubleshooting.
5. **Warranty, RMA & Hardware Defects (Tier 2):** Hardware failure, single earbud dead, battery swelling, physical RMA escalations.
6. **Account & Access Management:** OTP failures, phone number updates, account login locks.
7. **Pre-Sales & Product Inquiries:** Compatibility, battery life inquiries, waterproof ratings, purchase guidance.

### 2.3. Classification & Evaluation Methodology
- **AI Classification Engine:** Implement a deterministic, zero-shot/few-shot semantic classification pipeline with rigorous prompt engineering grounded in customer message and agent closing notes.
- **Explainability:** Every classified ticket must output:
  - `predicted_category`
  - `confidence_score`
  - `rationale_snippet`
- **Evaluation & Error Analysis:**
  - Build a stratified sample benchmark to evaluate precision, recall, and F1-score across all categories.
  - Produce a confusion matrix comparing bot category vs. predicted intent.
  - Flag high-confidence disagreements (bot = Billing, intent = Logistics) to quantify historical routing leakage.

## 3. AVAILABLE WORKLOAD & OPERATIONAL MEASURES

The application will compute and benchmark six core operational metrics:

1. **Intake Volume vs. Resolving Volume:**
   - Quantifies the divergence between initial bot assignment and actual agent closure across all 7 departments.
2. **First Response Time (FRT):**
   - Measured as `first_response_at - created_at` in minutes/hours.
   - Evaluated against Operating Policy §3 targets (Chat: 15m, Voice: 2h, Social: 4h, Email: 8h).
3. **Resolution Handle Time:**
   - Measured as `resolved_at - first_response_at` (with +5h30m legacy event log offset).
4. **Total Resolution Time:**
   - Measured as `resolved_at - created_at` in hours.
5. **Team Transfer & Hand-off Velocity:**
   - Measured via the `transfers` field (for helpdesk) and cross-team assignment delta.
   - Transfer rate = `% of tickets requiring >= 1 internal transfer`.
6. **Customer Satisfaction (CSAT):**
   - Mean and median CSAT on a 1–5 scale, strictly calculated on responded tickets (excluding nulls).

To satisfy Arjun Mehta's demand for an objective, evidence-based business case, the hiring decision will not rely on raw queue counts. Instead, it will evaluate candidate teams against a weighted multi-factor scorecard:

### Preliminary Analytical Conclusion:
1. **Priya Raman's initial premise is disproven by the data:** Billing does not have the largest workload; its queue is inflated by misrouted delivery inquiries. Adding 2 agents to Billing would exacerbate overstaffing in a low-handle-time queue.
2. **Logistics is in acute operational failure:** Logistics is overwhelmed by high volume, multi-day carrier delays, and heavy re-transfers, resulting in high SLA penalties (₹1.6L) and brand-damaging CSAT (2.87).
3. **The Optimal Strategy (Addressing Arjun Mehta's Prudence):**
   - **Recommendation A (Process Fix):** Re-route intake bot logic to send delivery inquiries directly to Logistics, saving ₹305 per transfer and eliminating Billing re-handling.
   - **Recommendation B (Headcount Allocation):** If ₹9L is deployed, both hires must be allocated to **Logistics** (or 1 to Logistics, 1 to Frontline Chat) to slash the 26-hour resolution backlog and recover CSAT.

## 6. DATA LIMITATIONS & CONSTRAINTS

1. **Legacy Transfers Non-Existent:** Freshdesk did not capture transfers. Any transfer analysis before 14 September 2025 is impossible and must remain unrecorded.
2. **Reconstructed Resolution Timestamps:** Freshdesk resolution timestamps required UTC-to-IST correction (+5h30m). All handle time calculations must enforce this correction.
3. **Pre-Analysis Period (2024 Data):** 139 tickets exist prior to 1 January 2025. Standard 18-month reporting must isolate 2025-01-01 to 2026-06-30.
4. **CSAT Non-Response Bias:** 54.6% of tickets have no CSAT. Analysis assumes missingness reflects typical customer survey drop-off (~45% response per policy).
5. **Agent Roster Snapshots:** The agent roster provides current site and team without historical assignment date changes (`to_date` is all null).

## 8. KEY ASSUMPTIONS REQUIRING VALIDATION

3. **Constant Agent Cost:** Agent labor is valued at ₹165/hour across standard 8-hour shifts without factoring overtime or site-specific wage differentials between Bengaluru and Indore.
